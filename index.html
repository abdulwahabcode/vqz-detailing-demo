Services

Detail Packages & Services

From maintenance washes to full transformations — every service done by hand, with pro-grade products.

/ 01

Full Interior Detail

Deep vacuum, steam-cleaned upholstery, leather cleaned and conditioned, vents, crevices and glass — the cabin feels new again.

/ 02

Exterior Wash & Wax

Two-bucket hand wash, wheels and tires deep-cleaned and dressed, finished with a durable spray wax for months of gloss.

Clay Bar Decontamination

Clay treatment pulls embedded road grime and industrial fallout from the paint, leaving it glass-smooth before wax or sealant.

Headlight Restoration

Cloudy, yellowed headlights sanded, polished and sealed — brighter night driving and a sharper front end.

Engine Bay Cleaning

Careful degrease and dressing of the engine bay — no damage, just a clean engine that looks as good as it runs.

/ 06

Ceramic Spray Sealant

A long-lasting ceramic spray coating for serious water beading and paint protection — the easy upgrade that keeps working.
